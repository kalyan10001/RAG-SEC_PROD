Compute Match — NCP (Provider) Guide

This guide is for NVIDIA Cloud Providers (NCPs): organizations that publish GPU capacity and respond to customer reservation requests.

NCP menu

Menu item Route Needs scope
Dashboard /provider/dashboard —
Upload Capacities /provider/gpu-capacities-upload manage_inventory
Manage reservation /provider?view=split approver_manage_reservation_request
Reservation requests /provider?view=table approver_view_reservation_request
Custom requests /provider/custom-requests custom request scopes (usually admins)

There is no sidebar and no in-page back button on the main NCP pages; navigate with the account menu.

Dashboard

Route: /provider/dashboard. A read-only overview that loads fresh on each visit.

  • Welcome header with your name and how many requests await your action.
  • Awaiting Your Action — requests waiting for accept, reject, counter or contract.
  • Available Capacities — your live inventory plus your latest upload status.
  • Quick links: View all requests (table view), Manage Reservations (split view), View all uploads, Upload Capacity.
  • Before your first upload, both panels show a guided empty state.

Upload Capacities (CSV)

Route: /provider/gpu-capacities-upload.

  1. Click Download Template.
  2. Fill one row per capacity block.
  3. Drag and drop or click to upload the CSV.
  4. You see Upload Successful; processing continues in the background and appears in Upload History.

CSV rules

  • UTF-8 CSV, at most 10 MB and 10,000 data rows, each cell at most 500 characters.
  • Required columns: GPU Platform Type, Number of GPUs Available, Live Token Date, Country, City.
  • Optional columns: Region, end_datetime.
  • Metadata columns that are accepted but ignored: NCP Name, RA/RD, Exemplar Cluster, aaS Capability, State.
  • The hosting provider name comes from your NCP organization, not from the CSV.
  • Vendor prefixes like “NVIDIA” in the platform cell are tolerated; country names such as “USA” or “Holland” are normalized.
  • Cells beginning with =, +, - or @ are neutralized to prevent spreadsheet formula injection.
  • Invalid rows are skipped with errors listed; the job fails only if no row succeeds.

Current versus future capacity

  • Rows whose Live Token Date is within 90 days of upload are current (AVAILABLE) capacity and go live immediately after validation.
  • Rows starting more than 90 days out are future (FUTURE) capacity. The job waits in Pending Review until a Platform Admin accepts or rejects it. You are emailed the result and any rejection reason appears in the Details column.

Upload History statuses

Processing, Pending Review, Completed, Rejected, Validation Failed. Click “See all details” for the full error list.

After uploading you may be asked “How was the Upload Capacity experience?”.

Manage reservation (split view)

Route: /provider?view=split. Your main workspace.

  • Left panel: request cards with status tabs (Awaiting NCP Acceptance, Awaiting Provider Approval, In Process, Contracted, Rejected, Cancelled, Expired) and filters for Platform, Geographical Region and Country, plus search.
  • Right panel: a capacity availability chart once Platform, Region and Country are all selected, so you can see whether you have room before acting.
  • Drag cards to reorder priority (keyboard: Space or Enter to grab, arrows to move).
  • Expand View details to see dates: In Process Since, Contracted On, Rejected On, Expired On, Cancelled On.

Actions on a card

Action When Effect
Accept Awaiting NCP Acceptance Offering moves to In Process; a 45-day contract window starts; customer receives an introduction email
Reject Awaiting acceptance or In Process Offering moves to Rejected; remarks are required and shown to the customer
Counteroffer Before contract Opens Modify Details: original request on the left, editable Proposed Start Date, Proposed End Date, Number of GPUs, Platform and Geographical Region on the right. Reset, Cancel or Confirm
Confirm Contract In Process Offering becomes Contracted, capacity is allocated, the whole request is Contracted and other NCPs on the request are cancelled as superseded

Accepting or contracting can fail with “capacity not available” if your remaining capacity no longer covers the request.

Reservation requests (table view)

Route: /provider?view=table. A sortable table with GPU Model, Country, Geographical Region, Status and sort filters. To act, choose Platform, Region and Country and click Manage Reservation to open the split view with those filters. Basic NCP users only get this view.

Reservation detail

Route: /provider/reservations/{reservationId}. Read-only Reservation Request History with Requested By, dates, region, platform, GPUs, capacity, total GPU hours, and price and cost when enabled.

Deadlines you must watch

  • 7 days to accept after a request is routed to you. Reminder 3 days before. If you miss it the offering becomes Expired.
  • 45 days from acceptance to confirm the contract. Reminder 3 days before.
  • Platform Admins can extend either window.
  • Magic links expire after 1 hour; ask for a resend within 7 days of routing, or just log in and open Manage reservation.

Emails NCPs receive

Magic link (new request), resend, introduction (after accept), contracted, superseded (another NCP won), expiry reminders, expired, request cancelled by customer, future capacity approved or rejected, welcome email for new users.
