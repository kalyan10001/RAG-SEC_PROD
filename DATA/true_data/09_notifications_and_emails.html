Compute Match — Email Notifications

Emails are rendered by the backend (backend/infrastructure/driven/email/, templates in templates/*.html, subjects in subjects.json). Most are triggered by Temporal workflow activities calling the backend’s System notification APIs (/api/v1/notification/*, scope notification:send).

Email When

Offering rejected An NCP rejected your request (with remarks)

Magic links in detail

  • Recipients: users of the NCP organization with role provider_business_user and in group offering-validator. Basic users never receive them.
  • The backend asks the Keycloak extension endpoint POST /realms/{realm}/clustruffle/create-action-token to mint an action token containing the offering id and a nonce.
  • When clicked, Keycloak validates the nonce with the backend, logs the user in and redirects with from=magic-link.
  • Valid 1 hour; resend possible for 7 days after routing.
  • Magic-link emails use the critical retry policy (10 attempts over about 25 minutes) because they are the only way an NCP learns about a request.

Reliability

  • Ordinary notifications retry 5 times over about 2.5 minutes.
  • Permanent failures are absorbed (and counted in metric clustruffle_workflow_activity_abandoned) so a workflow replay never re-sends email to users.
