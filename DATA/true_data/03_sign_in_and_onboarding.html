Compute Match — Signing Up, Signing In and First-Time Setup

How sign-in works

Compute Match uses Starfleet, NVIDIA’s single sign-on service, through Keycloak. Sign up and Login use the same flow.

  1. Open the home page and click Get Started, Sign up or Login.
  2. On the /login page enter your Work email and click Login.
  3. Personal email domains (Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail and similar) are blocked with an explanation. Use your work or enterprise email.
  4. You are redirected to Starfleet and your organization’s identity provider (for example NVIDIA corporate login or your company SSO).
  5. After authentication you return to Compute Match. You may briefly pass through several authentication pages; this is normal.

Technically, the frontend uses NextAuth 5 with the Keycloak client customer-web in the customer realm, and passes kc_idp_hint=starfleet to send users to the Starfleet identity broker.

Where you land after sign-in

Persona Landing page
Customer Find a Cluster to Reserve (/customer/reservation-request/available-clusters)
NCP Dashboard (/provider/dashboard)
Platform Admin Global Capacity Explorer (/provider/global-capacity) when available, otherwise the provider dashboard

First-time checks

After the first sign-in you may see one or more of these screens:

  • Complete Your Profile (customers, /customer/additional-user-details) or Complete Your NCP Profile (NCPs, /provider/additional-user-details). Fill First Name, Last Name, Organization Name and Phone Number (digits only; +1 is pre-filled) and click Submit.
  • Terms and Conditions modal. Scroll to the end, tick the checkbox and click Accept & Continue, or Sign out. Acceptance is stored as a timestamp on your user (POST /api/v1/users/me/terms-acceptance).
  • Thanks for Signing up (/user-status-pending). Your account is pending activation; you will get an email when activated.
  • Your access is being reviewed (/access-pending). Your company is an NVIDIA partner but its NCP organization is not onboarded yet. A Platform Admin must approve your access request. Once approved, the page shows Your access has been approved while the session refreshes.
  • Access denied (/access-denied). Your identity was verified but you are not authorized. The page shows your email and the reason. Contact computematchmake@nvidia.com or sign out and use another work account.

NCP sign-in paths

Welcome email

New NCP users created by NCP onboarding receive a provider_welcome email with a direct login link.

Magic link (respond to a reservation)

When a reservation request is routed to an NCP, users in that NCP organization who have the provider_business_user role and belong to the offering-validator group get an email with a magic link.

  1. The link is a Keycloak action token created by the custom accept-reservation-token extension.
  2. Keycloak validates the token and a one-time nonce with the backend (POST /api/v1/offering/validate-nonce), blocks the link if a different user is already logged in, creates a session and redirects.
  3. The browser lands on /automatic-login?from=magic-link&callbackUrl=..., which skips the email page and reuses the SSO session, then opens Manage reservation so the NCP can Accept.
  4. A magic link is valid for 1 hour. NCPs or admins can resend one (POST /api/v1/provider/offering/{offeringId}/resend-magic-link) within 7 days of routing.

NPN portal

NVIDIA Partner Network (NPN) members can reach Compute Match directly from the NPN portal (feature flag NPN_PORTAL_INTEGRATION_ENABLED). They still pass through the work-email page and Starfleet. If their partner organization is not onboarded yet, an access request with source NPN_PORTAL is created and they wait on the access-pending page.

How partner detection works

  • Compute Match keeps a partner domain mirror of email domains belonging to NVIDIA partners, synced from Salesforce twice a day.
  • Domain lookup statuses are PARTNER, NOT_PARTNER and PENDING.
  • Personal mail providers can never be partner domains.
  • When a user from a partner domain signs up and their NCP is not onboarded, POST /api/v1/user creates an access request (statuses PENDING, APPROVED, REJECTED; sources NPN_PORTAL, SIGNUP).
  • A Platform Admin approves or rejects it under Onboard NCPs → Review Onboarding Requests. Approval provisions the organization and user in Keycloak; rejection emails the reason. A rejected applicant can apply again, which creates a new request.

Signing out

Use Logout in the account menu. If your session cannot be refreshed, the app shows a recovery screen and performs a full Keycloak logout.
