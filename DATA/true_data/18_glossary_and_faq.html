Frequently asked questions

How do I sign up? Click Sign up or Login, enter your work email, and sign in through Starfleet. Personal emails are not allowed.

Why do I see “Access denied”? Your identity was verified but your account is not authorized under current policies. Contact computematchmake@nvidia.com or sign in with another work account.

Why do I see “Your access is being reviewed”? Your company is an NVIDIA partner but its NCP organization is not on the platform yet. A Platform Admin must approve your access request.

How many NCPs can I send one request to? Up to 3 in the UI by default (backend allows up to 10), if you have the multi-NCP permission.

Will I get several contracts if I send to several NCPs? No. The options are alternatives; the first NCP to confirm the contract wins and the others are cancelled.

How long does an NCP have to respond? 7 days to accept, then 45 days to contract. Reminders arrive 3 days before each deadline. Admins can extend both.

My magic link says it expired. Links last 1 hour. Ask for a resend (possible for 7 days after routing) or log in normally and open Manage reservation.

Can I accept an NCP counteroffer as a customer? The customer detail page is view and cancel only; the NCP confirms the contract after you agree with them directly.

When can I cancel? While the request is Awaiting NCP Acceptance, awaiting approval, In Process or Expired; custom requests while Open or In Review.

What if no cluster matches my needs? Submit a custom request. Admins will review and mark it Matched or Unserviceable.

My CSV upload shows Pending Review. It contains future capacity (starting more than 90 days out); a Platform Admin must accept it.

My CSV upload shows Validation Failed. Open “See all details” for row errors. Check the five required columns, UTF-8 encoding, 10 MB and 10,000-row limits.

Why can’t I see Upload Capacities or Manage reservation? You lack the scope (manage_inventory or approver_manage_reservation_request). Basic NCP users are read-only. Ask your Platform Admin.

I have customer and NCP access. How do I switch? Use Switch profile to at the top of the account menu.

Where is the user manual? Click the book icon in the header; it opens the manual filtered to your role. The ? icon shows help for the current page.

What does What’s New show? Release highlights for your persona. A banner appears on your landing page for about 7 days; dismissing it keeps it under the bell icon. Use Mark All as Read to clear the unread dot.

How do I give feedback? Use Share Feedback in the header, or answer the star rating after creating a request or uploading capacity.
