Compute Match — Platform Admin Guide

Platform Admins are NVIDIA staff (roles such as platform_business_admin, platform_business_admin_super_user, platform_business_admin_ops_user) who run the marketplace. They use the same /provider/* pages as NCPs, with extra pages and cross-NCP visibility. The header shows the persona label Platform Admin.

Global Capacity Explorer

A read-only world view of all GPU capacity across NCPs.

  • Filters: Search, Platform (required for the chart), Geographical Region, NCP, Country / Data Centre Location, Start Date, End Date.
  • Map | List toggle. The map shows capacity hotspots; “No datacenter locations match the current filters.” when empty.
  • Available Capacity chart over time, colour-coded per NCP (same colour for current and future). Tooltips show the month’s peak-day total, current and future split, and per-city breakdown.
  • Export to Excel and Export to CSV.
  • API: GET /api/v1/provider/global-capacity-map.

Review Future Capacities

NCP uploads that include rows starting more than 90 days out wait here.

  • Tabs: All, Pending, Accepted, Rejected. Filter by NCP.
  • Columns: NCP, File Name (click for details), Uploaded Date, Status, Actions.
  • Accept: confirm in Approve Future Capacity Submission; status becomes Accepted and capacity goes live for customers.
  • Reject: enter a reason (required, up to 500 characters) and click Submit Rejection; the NCP is emailed the reason.
  • Nothing is accepted or rejected automatically; submissions wait until reviewed.

Onboard NCPs

  1. Download the NCP onboarding CSV template.
  2. Fill in the NCP organizations and users.
  3. Upload. A background Temporal ProviderOnboardingWorkflow validates the file, creates Keycloak organizations, users, roles and the offering-validator group membership, and sends welcome emails.
  4. Watch status in the history table. Onboarding job statuses: QUEUED, VALIDATING, VALIDATION_FAILED, ONBOARDING, COMPLETED, FAILED.

Review Onboarding Requests (access requests)

When the NPN portal integration is on, partner users whose NCP is not onboarded appear here. Approve provisions them like a CSV onboarding; Reject requires a reason and emails it. API: /api/v1/provider/access-requests (+ /approve, /reject).

Custom requests

Route: /provider/custom-requests. Table only (no split view, no NCP filter). Filter by Platform, Country, Geographical Region, Status, Sort.

Lifecycle you control: - Open → In Review (you are working on it) - In Review or Open → Matched (Mark as Matched / Confirm Match, optionally record the NCP, NCP region and a comment) - → Unserviceable (Close as Unserviceable) - Customers alone can Cancel while Open or In Review. - Matched, Unserviceable and Cancelled are final; a request never returns to Open. The customer is emailed on each change.

Reservation requests across all NCPs

The table view shows all requests platform-wide. Use the row action menu (⋮) to extend deadlines:

Action Applies to Effect
Extend Acceptance Duration Awaiting NCP Acceptance (or recently expired) Adds days to the NCP’s acceptance window
Extend Contract Negotiation Duration In Process Adds days to the contract window

Enter 1 to 200 extra days, check the previewed new expiry date, and click Confirm. Extending can revive an Expired offering back to its prior active status.

User operations

Operations users (operations role) can activate pending users, list users, resend activation magic links and delete users through the Operations APIs (/api/v1/... under the Operations tag).

Daily alert email

Every day at 09:00 UTC a Temporal schedule (in-process-requests-alert-daily) emails Platform Admins a digest of In Process offerings approaching their contract deadline.
